# Shortcuts

The Shortcuts module gives players one in-game panel for discovering
BenheimQoL's controls and passive features.

## Current Behavior

- `F8` shows or hides the panel unless the player is typing in a text field or
  console.
- The title shows the loaded BenheimQoL version.
- The panel lists active controls and passive features without changing
  gameplay.

## In Development

- No changes are currently in development.

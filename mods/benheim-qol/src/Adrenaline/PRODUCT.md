# Adrenaline

The Adrenaline module explains successful perfect defenses and the native
meter's decay timing. It does not change adrenaline balance.

## Behavior

- Perfect parries and perfect dodges show yellow `+N` feedback above the player.
- Ordinary blocks and rolls show no adrenaline feedback.
- The native meter shows a small countdown before and during decay.
- The countdown disappears at zero and leaves the native full-meter effect
  unchanged.

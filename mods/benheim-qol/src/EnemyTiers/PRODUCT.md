# Enemy Tiers

Enemy Tiers extends Valheim's star levels and creature behavior to increase
encounter variation, game depth, and skill-based combat. Stars and future alpha
monsters can carry creature-specific mechanical and AI differences while
remaining recognizable Valheim enemies.

## Direction

- Use stars and alphas as familiar signals for deeper creature behavior.
- Keep each variation coherent with the creature instead of drawing from a
  generic pool of unrelated powers.
- Let tiers vary mechanics and AI, not only health and damage.

No exact tier rules, alpha rules, creature behaviors, or AI changes are decided
yet.

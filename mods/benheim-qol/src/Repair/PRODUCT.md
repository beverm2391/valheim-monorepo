# Repair

The Repair module removes repeated repair clicks without changing the normal
station, stamina, durability, or access requirements.

## Behavior

- A normal station repair click keeps Valheim's one-item behavior.
- `Left Shift` + station repair click repairs all eligible gear.
- A normal hammer repair click keeps Valheim's one-piece behavior.
- `Left Shift` + hammer repair click repairs damaged accessible pieces near the
  selected building piece.

## Status

- **In development:** Mass building repair does not currently work in gameplay.
- **Needs test:** Repair-all gear, normal one-item gear repair, and normal
  one-piece building repair.

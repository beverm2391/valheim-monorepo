# Greydwarf Resident

A greydwarf friend lives at the base as a cozy, lore-oriented resident.
Actually lounging in the hot tub is essential to the first feature.

This is part of Ben's taming and cozy/cosmetic direction: extend the feeling
of a base inhabited by creatures. The resident is not a taming progression
system or a worker.

## In Development

Explore the seated greydwarf outside the Bog Witch's hut as the native donor
for the resident's appearance and pose. Reuse depends on inspecting the actual
asset and seeing the result in a hot tub.

The first outcome is a visible prototype in the authorized disposable local
Lab world. It must look like a greydwarf relaxing in the tub. Asset reuse and
the hot-tub presentation remain unproven until inspected in-game.

How players place or invite the resident, persistence, and multiplayer behavior
remain open for the shipped feature. The Lab prototype does not establish
those behaviors.

## Later

Other fun behaviors or states may follow the hot-tub resident. Their scope is
not yet decided.

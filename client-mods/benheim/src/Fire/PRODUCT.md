# Smoke-Blocked Fires

Fires with fuel should not blink off because their smoke outlet is blocked.

## Current Behavior

- A fueled hearth keeps burning when its smoke outlet is blocked.

## In Development

- Other native fire pieces should keep burning when smoke blockage alone would
  shut them off. Fuel, water, rain, terrain, cover, and other native burn
  conditions should still apply.
- Benheim should not suppress or reroute smoke. Smoke should still damage
  players.

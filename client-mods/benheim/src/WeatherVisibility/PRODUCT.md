# Blizzard Visibility

Mountain snowstorms can make Frost Cave entrances almost impossible to spot.
Benheim offers a client-side visibility option so a player can keep exploring
through a blizzard.

## In Development

- The player can turn Blizzard Visibility on or off while playing. It reduces
  the snow and mist effects responsible for the Mountain whiteout enough to
  make terrain and cave entrances readable at a useful scouting distance.
- The Mountain still looks and sounds stormy. Wind, weather timing, cold and
  freezing effects, and every other gameplay consequence remain native.
- The setting changes only that player's view. It does not change the server's
  weather, other players' views, world data, or clear-weather scenes.
- Tune the effect against Valheim 1.0 in a real Mountain blizzard. The exact
  particles, fog contribution, and control presentation follow that proof.

The [Blizzard Clear Textures mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/valheim/mods/1788)
is a behavior reference: it hides blizzard snow by replacing a texture and
leaves misty gusts. It was last updated in 2022; its current-game behavior is
unproven. Benheim will own its own client-side control and visual treatment.

## Status

The desired behavior is agreed. No Benheim implementation or gameplay proof
has landed yet.
